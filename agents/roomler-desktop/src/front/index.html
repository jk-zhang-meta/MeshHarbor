    Your private network
    
            
    
              Roomler can join this computer to a private network with your
              other devices. It is a WireGuard mesh: your devices connect to
              each other with end-to-end encryption, directly when the network
              allows it and through a relay when it doesn't. The Roomler server
              introduces them to each other but never sees what they send, and
              a relay only ever carries encrypted data.
            
    
            
      
              
    • 
                What you get. Reach your devices by name. Remote
                desktop, SSH and tunnels use this network.
              
      • 
              
    • 
                What changes on this computer. Roomler adds a
                virtual network adapter, with routes for the network's own
                addresses (in the 100.64.0.0/10 range). If your admins have
                approved a subnet router (an office network reachable through
                one of your devices, say), its addresses go through the private
                network too. Your internet traffic is not rerouted.
              
      • 
              
    • 
                Who can reach this computer. Only devices in your
                organisation, and only as far as your organisation's access rules
                allow. When your admins enforce rules, anything they have not
                allowed is blocked.
              
      • 
              
    • 
                Turning it off. Settings → Private network, at
                any time.
